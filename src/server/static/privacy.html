Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 18, 2026 | Last Updated: November 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how GTA 6 - Vice Desk Intelligence ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you connect or use our application with Meta and Instagram Graph APIs.

Summary: We only access the data required to publish and analyze authorized gaming video reels and shorts on behalf of verified content creator accounts. We never sell your data or share it with unauthorized third parties.

1. Information We Collect

When you authenticate your Instagram or Meta account through our application, we may receive:

  • Account Identifiers: Instagram Business Account ID and Facebook Page ID.
  • Media & Publishing Permissions: Access tokens strictly required to upload videos, generate reels containers, and check media publishing status.
  • Performance Insights: Public engagement metrics (view count, likes) to evaluate video content performance.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information solely for:

  • Automating video reel rendering and publishing to authorized Instagram creator accounts.
  • Monitoring container processing status via Meta Graph API v19.0 webhooks.
  • Providing performance analytics and content optimization feedback.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain access tokens only for the duration needed to service your account. You can request complete deletion of your data at any time by visiting our Data Deletion Instructions Page or by emailing our data protection contact.

4. Security

All communication with Meta Graph APIs is encrypted using TLS 1.3. Access tokens are stored securely in protected environment variables and are never exposed publicly.
